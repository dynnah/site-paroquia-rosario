# Page section (`SecaoPagina`)

A home-page section: eyebrow, serif title (with an optional italic part in `mariano`) and, on the right, a "see all" ghost button.

- Breathing room between sections: **48px on the phone, 64px from desktop** (`ros-secao`).
- `ros-secao--afundada` gives the band the `fundo-afundado` background, to alternate with sections on the normal background.
- The header wraps on narrow screens: the button drops below the title.
- The section is labelled by its title (`aria-labelledby`). Put a rosary divider between big sections.
