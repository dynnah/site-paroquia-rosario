# Community card (`Pastoral`)

Card for a pastoral, movement, group, ministry or project: what it is, when it meets, how to join.

- The coloured **4px strip** at the top says what kind of group it is. On the *Nossa Comunidade* page: `--pastoral` (dark brown, `marrom`) for pastorals and `--movimento` (a lighter brown) for movements, groups, ministries and projects. Other pages may use the family tones: default `acao` (parish), `--franciscano` (terracotta), `--mariano` (blue), `--social` (green: baskets, recycling).
- Always: name, one sentence about what it does, when it meets, how to get in touch and a button. The eyebrow (category) is optional.
- **The button sits at the foot of the card.** Cards in a row share the same height (the grid stretches them) and the body fills what is left, so the last item (the button) goes down to the bottom.
- In a `ros-grade`: 1 column on the phone, 2 on the tablet, 3 on desktop, with no manual breaks.
